ALETHEIA - READ-ONLY MAC INVESTIGATION KIT
==========================================

1. PREPARE (on YOUR computer, once, needs internet)
   - Format the USB stick as exFAT, name it EVIDENCE.
   - Copy this whole "aletheia" folder onto the stick  ->  EVIDENCE/aletheia/
   - Windows: open aletheia\setup and double-click SETUP_USB_Windows.bat
     Mac:     bash /Volumes/EVIDENCE/aletheia/setup/setup_usb_mac.command
   - Wait for "DONE". You should now see folders python-aarch64 and python-x86_64.

2. AT THE CLIENT'S MAC (with the owner's written permission)
   - System Settings > Privacy & Security > Full Disk Access > turn ON "Terminal".
   - Open Terminal, type:  bash /Volumes/EVIDENCE/aletheia/mac/run_mac_investigation.command
   - Answer the questions (put the suspect e-mail address at "Watch for").
   - Wait 10-40 min. Write down the "Manifest SHA-256". Quit Terminal, eject the USB.

3. ON YOUR COMPUTER
   - Open aletheia/cases/<case>/report/report.html  ->  section 1A answers Q1-Q9.
   - Print to PDF for the file.

Full instructions: Aletheia_Mac_User_Guide.pdf (in this folder).
Read-only: nothing on the Mac is deleted, changed, reset or installed.
